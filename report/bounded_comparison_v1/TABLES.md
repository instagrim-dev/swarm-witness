The LessWrong reproduction post and its appendix are one public record. They are not the original incident and not the twelve-history experiment. Unavailable original transcripts and platform logs are recorded in the lineage block and are not qualification gates.

Frozen public finding: Swarm Witness matches the strong checker on all 24 public cells. Unsupported supports are naive PASS 24, strong checker 0, Swarm Witness 0. The comparison does not establish superiority.

Unsupported attribution is counted over every scored cell, including cells whose warranted origin is undetermined. A concrete origin that is not the warranted origin counts. An undetermined assignment is an abstention. Correct attribution coverage is correct concrete-origin assignments divided by cells whose warranted origin is uniquely determined. An always-undetermined method scores 0 when that denominator is positive. A zero denominator is not applicable, and the raw counts are kept.

The earlier staged comparison remains in its own table. It is author-curated. Mechanical withholding of later-stage evidence is not independent curation. That table is not replaced by the strong-checker tie.
